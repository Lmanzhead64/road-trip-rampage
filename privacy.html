Road Trip Rampage Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 6, 2026

Road Trip Rampage is a fictional vehicle defense game. The game stores gameplay progress and local high scores on your device using browser storage.

Data We Store

The game may save your level, score, upgrades, selected vehicle, village progress, and high-score name locally on your device. Local save data stays on your device unless you choose to submit a leaderboard score.

Optional Online Leaderboard

If the online leaderboard is enabled and you choose to submit a score, the game may send your display name, score, level reached, game mode, win streak, tank tier, combat rating, selected skin name, game version, and submission time to the leaderboard service. Leaderboard entries may be visible to other players. Do not enter private personal information as your display name.

Tracking

The current build does not use advertising trackers, analytics SDKs, location tracking, contacts, camera, microphone, or third-party sign-in.

Deleting Leaderboard Entries

If you want a submitted leaderboard entry removed, contact the developer through the support page with the display name and approximate submission date.

Children

The current prototype is intended for a general classroom/project audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, contact the developer through the project repository or the support page.
