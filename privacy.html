Road Trip Rampage Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 5, 2026

Road Trip Rampage is a fictional vehicle defense game. This prototype stores gameplay progress and local high scores on your device using browser storage.

Data We Store

The game may save your level, score, upgrades, selected vehicle, village progress, and high-score name locally on your device. This data is not sent to a server in the current build.

Tracking

The current build does not use advertising trackers, analytics SDKs, location tracking, contacts, camera, microphone, or third-party sign-in.

Children

The current prototype is intended for a general classroom/project audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, contact the developer through the project repository or the support page.
