Road Trip Rampage 3D

Command an Abrams-inspired fictional battle tank through a 100-level city campaign that starts in New York City, then shifts to a new U.S. or international city every ten levels. Lock the closest enemy armor, recover resupply crates with your support runner, upgrade your vehicle and home base, then face a commander boss at the end of every level. Built by Levi Ray for Games.

Closest-Enemy Lock Auto Aim and Auto Shoot prioritize the nearest hostile tank first. Bright tracer fire shows every shot path.
Resupply Loop Destroyed enemy armor can drop field supplies for hull repair, credits, shell reloads, and missile cycling.
Garage + Base Combat upgrades raise power. Cosmetic skins are separate, and base upgrades improve repairs and turret cover.
