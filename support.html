Road Trip Rampage Support

Road Trip Rampage is a fictional armored vehicle defense RPG prototype.

How To Play

Move with WASD or arrow keys. The cannon auto-aims. Press Q to launch demo charges. Press Space or M for missiles after unlocking them. On touchscreen, drag the left control to move and tap DEMO or MISSILE.

Progress

Progress and high scores are saved locally on the device. Clearing browser data may reset saved progress.

Contact

For help, use the GitHub repository issue tracker or contact the developer through the class/project submission channel.
